SentinelGel/Project

Relief that sits where reflux hurts

Who SentinelGel is for, and what it is meant to change for them.

Who it is for

About 1 in 7 people worldwide live with gastroesophageal reflux disease.

For many of them the damage is not only in the stomach. Refluxed acid and pepsin reach the esophagus and the throat, and show up as pain, hoarseness and a cough that does not go away.

SentinelGel is designed for that part of the problem: the tissue above the stomach, where today's treatments do the least.

What it is meant to change

  1. 1

    Protection at the site of injury

    The gel coats the throat and esophagus themselves, instead of working only in the stomach.

  2. 2

    A response, not just a barrier

    The buffer is released when the pH drops, at the place the acid lands.

  3. 3

    Nothing new to learn

    It is a drink. It needs no device, no timing chart and no special technique.

  4. 4

    No gas

    Magnesium hydroxide neutralizes acid without the CO₂ that can push more reflux up.

Built so children will take it

A treatment only helps if it is swallowed. SentinelGel is a thin liquid, sweetened with sucralose or stevia and flavored with vanilla or banana.

It leaves out mint, a common flavor in stomach remedies, because mint relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter.

Made from familiar materials

Every component is used in food or medicine already, or is a modified version of one. That keeps the path from idea to a testable product as short and as safe as we can make it.

What we still have to show

The impact described here is the intended one. SentinelGel is a concept in development, and each claim has a bench test attached to it on the testing page, along with the questions we cannot answer yet.

Please noteSentinelGel is not a medical product, and nothing on this site is medical advice. If you have reflux symptoms, talk to a doctor.
