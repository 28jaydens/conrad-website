§ 2Our answer

SentinelGel

One liquid carries a passive shield and active responders, so it protects all the time and reacts during reflux.

SHIELDING

A gel layer that physically blocks acid and pepsin from the lining.

ADHESIVE

Mussel-inspired catechol–hyaluronic acid grips wet mucosa.

RESPONSIVE

Buffer beads stay closed at saliva pH and open below about pH 5.

GENTLE

Mg(OH)₂ neutralizes acid without making CO₂ gas, and the coat sheds on its own.

DRINKABLE

A thin, flavored liquid at room temperature that gels at body temperature.

§ 3Method

How SentinelGel works

From sip to protection, in five steps.

Local pH7.0Before the sip

Fig. 3The coating, in cross-section

01

Drink it

A thin, flavored liquid at room temperature. Nothing to chew, nothing chalky.

02

It coats

The liquid warms to 37 °C, gels and sticks to the throat and esophagus. The buffer beads sit inside it, closed.

03

Reflux hits

Acid reaches the coated surface and the pH there drops below about 5.

04

Beads open

The beads swell and release Mg(OH)₂, which neutralizes the acid. The higher pH slows pepsin, and the charged beads cling harder to the mucus.

05

Sheds safely

The coat leaves over a few hours as the surface cells naturally renew.

§ 5Mechanism

Acid protonates chitosan, the bead swells, and the buffer comes out where it is needed

  1. 1

    Hold the buffer

    Chitosan–alginate microspheres carry Mg(OH)₂ and stay collapsed at normal saliva pH.

  2. 2

    Sense the acid

    Chitosan's amine groups (pKa about 6.5) pick up protons as the pH falls.

  3. 3

    Swell open

    Like charges repel. The bead swells, its pores open, and it clings harder to negatively charged mucus.

  4. 4

    Neutralize

    Mg(OH)₂ takes up H⁺ without making CO₂, so there is no burp to push more reflux up.
